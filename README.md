# ♻️ E-Waste Management System ♻️
# Still Under Development

## Overview
The E-Waste Management System is a web-based platform developed to assist university students in managing electronic waste responsibly and efficiently. The system integrates disposal, repair, resale, consultation and feedback services into one centralized platform, promoting sustainability and environmental awareness.

## Objectives
- Reduce electronic waste generation in university.
- Encourage repair and reuse of electronic devices.
- Promote responsible disposal practices.
- Increase environmental awareness among users.
- Improve efficiency in e-waste management operations.

## Features

### E-Waste Disposal Management
- Submit disposal requests for damaged or obsolete devices.
- Track disposal status and records.

### Repair Request Module
- Request repairs for faulty electronic equipment.
- Monitor repair progress and outcomes.

### Resale Management
- List reusable electronic devices for resale.
- Extend device lifecycle and reduce waste.

### Consultation Service
- Seek expert advice regarding e-waste handling and sustainability practices.
- Access guidance on repair, disposal, and recycling options.

### Feedback System
- Submit feedback on services provided.
- Help improve system performance and user experience.

## Benefits
- Supports sustainable university operations.
- Reduces environmental impact caused by electronic waste.
- Encourages reuse and recycling practices.
- Provides centralized management of e-waste activities.
- Enhances environmental awareness among university communities.
